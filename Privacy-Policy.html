Privacy Policy for VeloStudio

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

At VeloStudio, we are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to the VeloStudio browser extension published on the Chrome Web Store. This document describes your rights regarding our collection, usage, and protection of your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Absolute Privacy)

Unlike traditional screen recorders, VeloStudio operates entirely on your local machine using client-side WebAssembly technology (FFmpeg.wasm). Your media files never touch any external servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

  • Audio and Video Files: We do not store, view, or have access to the screen recordings, camera feeds, or audio files captured through our extension. All rendering, trimming, cropping, and stitching operations are processed 100% locally in your browser memory.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a stable and optimized user experience, we may collect minimal, anonymized technical data:

  • Anonymized Usage Data: We may collect basic anonymized telemetry, such as extension activation counts, page views of the standalone editor page, and button clicks to enhance the performance of the software.
  • Technical Logs: Basic system information such as browser version, operating system, and error logs related to WebAssembly modules to troubleshoot crashes and bugs.

3. How We Use the Information We Collect

Our primary goal in analyzing non-personal usage data is to ensure a safe, efficient, and reliable user experience. We may use this technical data to:

  • Provide and maintain the quality of VeloStudio services.
  • Debug browser-specific memory allocations or compliance issues with Chrome's Content Security Policy (CSP).
  • Improve software functionalities and user interface responsiveness.

4. Information Collected by Third-Parties

We do not sell or distribute user data. However, we may utilize standard analytical tools like Google Analytics to obtain high-level application usage and operational diagnostics. If you wish to opt out of third-party analytics tracking, you can configure your browser to apply standard ad-blocking rules.

5. How We Protect and Store Personal Information

Since VeloStudio processes all core functions inside the extension's local origin environment, your high-resolution recorded chunks are safely isolated using standard browser data structures (IndexedDB). No data is transmitted cross-border or kept on permanent remote endpoints.

6. Children's Privacy

We strictly forbid the unauthorized use of our software by individuals under the age of 13. VeloStudio does not knowingly or intentionally collect, track, or request any personal identification data from children under the age of 13.

7. Notice of Changes to This Privacy Policy

In the event that we release updated versions of VeloStudio that introduce new workflows or advanced features requiring additional privacy disclosure, we will update this Privacy Policy page to support our revised business practices.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, security concerns, or technical inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or local data boundaries, please reach out to our team through the official support channel on the Chrome Web Store extension page.
